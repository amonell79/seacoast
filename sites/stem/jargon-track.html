Words 1–6

Audio
Lingo

The language of the music room — and of every podcast you've ever heard.

LOGO
Audio • 1 of 18

DAW

What do you think it means?

The software you record, arrange and mix a song in. Digital Audio Workstation.

FL Studio, Ableton, Logic, GarageBand — every one of those is a DAW.

LOGO
Audio • 2 of 18

BPM

What do you think it means?

Beats per minute. How fast the song moves.

A slow worship song sits around 70. A hype track is closer to 140.

LOGO
Audio • 3 of 18

Gain

What do you think it means?

How strong the signal is going in — set before you ever hit record.

Get it wrong at the start and no amount of fixing later saves it.

LOGO
Audio • 4 of 18

Clipping

What do you think it means?

Too much gain. The top of the wave gets cut off and it turns to distortion.

You cannot undo it. The part that got cut off was never recorded.

LOGO
Audio • 5 of 18

Stems

What do you think it means?

A song split into its separate parts — drums alone, vocals alone, bass alone.

You need stems to remix anything, or to turn one part down without touching the rest.

LOGO
Audio • 6 of 18

Bounce

What do you think it means?

Turning the whole project into one finished audio file.

Until you bounce it, nobody but you can hear it.

Words 7–12

Video
Lingo

What the people in the booth say to each other while a service is going out live.

LOGO
Video • 7 of 18

Switcher

What do you think it means?

The box every camera plugs into. It picks which camera the audience sees.

Ours is the RØDECaster Video. One tap changes the shot for everybody watching.

LOGO
Video • 8 of 18

A-cam / B-cam

What do you think it means?

A-cam is the main shot. B-cam is the second angle.

Our A-cam is the R5 C. Our B-cam is the R8.

LOGO
Video • 9 of 18

Headroom

What do you think it means?

The space between the top of someone's head and the top of the frame.

Too much and they look tiny. Too little and you have cut their head off.

LOGO
Video • 10 of 18

Sync

What do you think it means?

Audio and video lined up so the lips match the words.

Audio gets through the system faster, so you delay it until the picture catches up.

LOGO
Video • 11 of 18

Latency

What do you think it means?

The delay between something happening in this room and someone at home seeing it.

Measured in milliseconds. Chapel reaches the internet a few seconds behind real life.

LOGO
Video • 12 of 18

B-roll

What do you think it means?

Extra footage you cut over the top of someone talking.

It is the reason you are not staring at one face for eight straight minutes.

Words 13–18

Design & Photo
Lingo

The words behind every post, every thumbnail, every picture this school puts out.

LOGO
Design • 13 of 18

Thumbnail

What do you think it means?

The one image that decides whether anybody clicks the video.

Read in half a second, on a phone, at about the size of a stamp.

LOGO
Design • 14 of 18

Culling

What do you think it means?

Cutting four hundred photos down to the twelve that are actually good.

It is most of the job, and nobody ever sees you do it.

LOGO
Design • 15 of 18

RAW

What do you think it means?

A photo file that keeps everything the sensor saw, so you can fix it later.

A JPEG throws most of that away to save space — and you cannot get it back.

LOGO
Design • 16 of 18

Contrast

What do you think it means?

The distance between the darkest parts of an image and the lightest.

Low contrast looks flat and grey. High contrast punches off the screen.

LOGO
Design • 17 of 18

Brand kit

What do you think it means?

Your colors, your fonts and your logo saved in one place.

It is why every slide you have seen this week is the same navy and the same gold.

LOGO
Design • 18 of 18

Lower third

What do you think it means?

The name graphic that sits at the bottom of the screen during a broadcast.

Bottom third of the frame. That is the entire reason for the name.
